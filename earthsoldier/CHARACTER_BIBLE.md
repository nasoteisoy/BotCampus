# Earth Soldier — locked character bible

Reuse verbatim in every frame prompt. Same character as hero_ref.png. Do not redesign.

## Silhouette
- Stocky, wide-legged heavily armored infantry (same family as Fire/Water/Air Soldier)
- Broad shoulders, thick pauldrons, heavy boots
- Full-face stone helm with short rocky moss crest
- Round stone buckler + war hammer / maul silhouette

## Palette
- Armor: rough-hewn brown stone with mossy olive-green patches in cracks
- Chest: large circular amber-green stone-gem core
- Helm visor slit: glowing amber-lime green horizontal line
- Crest: short rocky brown/moss stone crest spikes (not fire, not water, not wind plume)
- Shield: heavy round stone buckler with stylized glowing green leaf/mountain emblem
- Hammer: thick stone war hammer/maul head with green crystal accent; dark wood/stone haft

## Signature parts (every frame)
1. Rocky moss crest on full-face stone helm
2. Glowing green visor slit
3. Circular amber-green gem chest core
4. Brown stone armor with moss accents
5. Round stone buckler + leaf/mountain emblem
6. Stone war hammer with green crystal accent

## Style / camera
- Cel-shaded Clash/AoE-readable game art, hard edges, high contrast
- Isometric-friendly high camera for AoE2-style play
- True transparent background; character only; no floor; no scenery; no text
- NO cape / cloak

## Facing notes
- SE = primary hero angle (matches ref)
- Unique art: N, NE, E, SE, S for idle + walk
- W / NW / SW = horizontal flip of E / NE / SE after QC
- Attack: SE primary

## Flow rules
- Idle: feet planted, hammer upright-ish or ready, shield close to body
- Walk A: left foot forward mid-stride; hammer/shield follow through from idle
- Walk B: opposite foot forward; continuous cycle with Walk A
- Neighbor dirs = small turn of SAME body, not a redesign

## Gear motion (mandatory)
- Hammer head/angle moves with weapon arm each frame (idle → walk A arc → walk B opposite phase)
- Round stone buckler tilts/translates with off-hand and gait
- No frozen gear; no teleport; gear always present; no appear/disappear mid-loop
