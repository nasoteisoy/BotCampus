Game rendering — research board notes

Updated: 2026-09-20 · Leader seed + Researcher A/B (live updates expected)

Topic: **game rendering** for Jesus’s soldiers + campus (Clash/Kingshot/mobile-ad readable)


What “game rendering” means here

Not film VFX. Production look that **reads on a phone**, survives clutter, and matches a locked art bible across idle/walk/attack.


Two pipelines we care about:

  • **Stylized 3D → sprites** (Clash-like): model/texture in 3D, render multi-angle frames to 2D.
  • **Direct 2D / iso sheets** (what we’re shipping now): paint/gen frames; still obey the same readability rules.

Industry bar (stylized mobile / Clash-adjacent)

Sources: Supercell Substance interview (Clash stylized PBR); stylized character production playbooks; Clash Royale readability writeups.


Must-haves

  • **Silhouette first** — recognizable in black at gameplay size (phone). Test idle + walk + attack.
  • **Exaggerated anchors** — crest, weapon, chest glow, shield emblem as identity locks (not tiny noise).
  • **Stylized PBR discipline** — simple albedo, roughness does the “juice”; metals rougher than real life; don’t bake full lighting into color.
  • **Value compression** — limited value range so characters don’t go muddy on OLED phones.
  • **Detail hierarchy** — detail on face/chest/weapon; rest quieter (detail everywhere = noise).
  • **Consistent lighting family** across every frame of a sheet (same key direction).
  • **Transparent clean plates** — no white boxes; crop to character; stable foot baseline across facings.

For our elemental soldiers (2D iso)

  • Same bible + hero ref every frame.
  • Walk: opposite feet A/B minimum; industry prefers 6–8 frames/dir — we’re at 2 until FLOW pass upgrades.
  • Weapon/shield **re-armed every frame** with continuous tip arc (no cardboard glue, no teleport).
  • Element color is identity, not a redesign (Fire/Water/Air/Earth = palette + VFX, same soldier language).
  • Phone review: Sprite Lab walk row must visibly flip A↔B; play page required.

Pitfalls (seen on our project)

  • Bubble/sphere primitive 3D that doesn’t match refs
  • White sprite backgrounds
  • Same-leg walk / skate
  • Frozen gear across walk frames
  • Over-detail that dies at map scale
  • Different lighting per facing (looks like different characters)

Checklist before Render ships

  • [ ] Silhouette reads at ~64–128px tall
  • [ ] Hero ref locked; bible pasted every gen
  • [ ] Idle→walk continuous stance
  • [ ] Opposite-foot walk (A≠B)
  • [ ] Weapon/shield natural motion
  • [ ] Clean alpha + shared height
  • [ ] Public preview strip + **play/review URL**

Iso RTS walk cycles (AoE2-like soldiers)

**Date:** 2026-09-20 · **Researcher A** · **Board topic:** rendering / walk cycles


Checklist (must-haves)

  • **8 frames per direction** (contact → down → pass → up × both feet). Economy: 6 if limbs still read; avoid 4 for full soldiers.
  • **Opposite feet** each half-cycle; arms contralateral.
  • **Weight bob** — lowest at contact, tallest at pass; pin foot-contact Y across facings.
  • **Playback ~8–12 FPS**; stride matched to sim speed (**no skating**).
  • **Walk: no soft smears**; clear limb silhouettes every frame.
  • **Held weapon:** re-arm after body; tip arc continuous with shoulder bob; elemental trail follows same arc.
  • **8 facings** (AoE2 bar); mirror L/R only when handedness survives.

Top pitfalls

  • Same-foot loop / skating
  • Cardboard glued weapon
  • Soft blur smears on walk
  • Uneven bob height per facing

Sources

  • Gamedev.SE — Diablo walk ≈ 8 frames (4 poses × 2 steps)
  • AoE2 forums — 8 angles; example sheets 16 frames/angle (unit-dependent)
  • SLYNYRD Pixelblog 50 — 8-frame sweet spot; contact/down/pass/up
  • Pixel art FPS guides — walk 8–12 FPS; contact dip sells weight
  • SLYNYRD weapon walks — body first, then held-weapon arms
