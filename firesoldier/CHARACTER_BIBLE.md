# Fire Soldier — locked character bible

Reuse verbatim in every frame prompt. Same character as hero_ref.png. Do not redesign.

## Silhouette
- Stocky, wide-legged heavily armored infantry
- Broad shoulders, thick pauldrons, heavy boots
- Full-face Corinthian-style helm with short upright red mohawk plume
- Round shield + long spear/polearm silhouette

## Palette
- Armor: dark charcoal / basalt gray stone plate
- Lava veins: bright neon orange cracks through all armor plates
- Chest: large circular molten furnace core (orange with yellow-white hot center)
- Helm visor slit: glowing orange horizontal line
- Crest plume: solid vibrant crimson red mohawk
- Shield: dark cracked stone with stylized glowing orange flame emblem
- Spearhead: translucent molten orange-to-red crystal glow; dark segmented shaft

## Signature parts (every frame)
1. Red mohawk plume on full-face helm
2. Glowing orange visor slit
3. Circular molten chest furnace core
4. Basalt armor with orange lava vein cracks
5. Round stone shield + flame emblem
6. Glowing molten spearhead

## Style / camera
- Cel-shaded Clash/AoE-readable game art, hard edges, high contrast
- Isometric-friendly high camera for AoE2-style play
- True transparent background; character only; no floor; no scenery; no text

## Facing notes
- SE = primary hero angle (matches ref)
- Unique art: N, NE, E, SE, S for idle + walk
- W / NW / SW = horizontal flip of E / NE / SE after QC (shield/spear mirrored carefully)
- Attack: SE primary (+ E, S if time)

## Flow rules (v2)
- Idle is the locked stance per facing: feet planted, spear upright-ish, shield close to body.
- Walk A = same facing, left foot forward mid-stride, spear/shield follow through from idle (not a new pose family).
- Walk B = opposite foot forward (right foot forward), continuous cycle with Walk A — same lean, same weapon angles family.
- Neighbor dirs must look like a small turn of the SAME body, not a redesign. Keep plume, chest core, shield emblem, lava veins identical.
- Keep spear closer to body on E profile to avoid extreme width.
