# Phone Roam / Combat — Options for Jesus / Profile

Dev A pack. Three distinct verbs + cameras. Pick one direction to flesh for production.

---

## A — Arena Clear (top-down twin-stick)

**One-liner:** Roam an arena with virtual twin-sticks, spray down waves, chase score.

**Feel:** Geometry Wars–lite / roam-and-clear. (Researcher A: fat sticks, 1–5 hit enemies, knife-edge waves, mute juice.) Left stick move, right stick or Fire button shoot. Light camera follow on the player. Mute-readable hit flash, scale punch, screen shake.

**Why pick it:** Clearest “phone twin-stick” prototype; wave loop readable in under two minutes.

**Play:** `a/index.html`

---

## B — Corridor Slash (side-view) · **Dev B**

**One-liner:** Side-scroll run and jump; slash is primary, gun is alt; carve a corridor.

**Feel:** Not twin-stick — ground-plane enemies approach, camera scrolls with the runner. Combo meter rewards aggressive clears. **Win: clear 6 waves** (Dev B owned).

**Why pick it:** Classic mobile side-fighter verbs; distinct silhouette and camera from A/C.

**Play:** `b/index.html`

---

## C — Shadow Takedown (iso stealth)

**One-liner:** Fake-iso diamond map; stay out of vision cones; dash in and takedown from behind.

**Feel:** AoE2-ish camera, hit-and-run stealth — alert meters on guards + global alert bar. No spray shooting; short dash + melee takedown when unseen/rear. Cover tiles help.

**Why pick it:** Different fantasy and pacing; stealth juice without copying A’s twin-stick or B’s platformer.

**Play:** `c/index.html`
