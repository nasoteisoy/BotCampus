# ☕ Dutch Bros · New Shop Opening Tracker

> **Pitch concept, unofficial.** Not affiliated with or endorsed by Dutch Bros. The name is used only to identify the brand. The windmill-on-a-cup mark, the icons and all the 3D art are original; no logo or brand artwork is copied. (Folder and storage keys keep the old `coffee-opening` name.)

A 3D tracker for opening a **new Dutch Bros-style double drive-thru stand**. An isometric lot builds itself up as the opening plan moves forward: survey stakes, then the foundation, the blue-and-white stand, the roof with its little windmill, the walk-up canopy, the two drive-thru lanes, equipment, Broistas, a car queue in each lane with line-buster Broistas on tablets, and balloons on opening day. The progress comes from your real checklists, which you can edit.

Live once pushed: https://nasoteisoy.github.io/BotCampus/games/coffee-opening/ (Add to Home Screen to install it; it works offline after the first visit.)

**🧪 Example data.** The 11 example shops (Tempe, Mesa, Phoenix, Fountain Hills, Peoria, Gilbert, Scottsdale, Chandler, Goodyear, Glendale, Surprise) are made-up Phoenix-area examples in mixed states, not real plans. ⚙ → 🧹 Clear examples (tap twice) removes only the 🧪 stores and keeps yours. ⚙ → ↺ resets everything back to the examples.

## v4: 📍 Phoenix metro map
- **📍 toggle** (right beside 🗺, or ⚙ → 📍): a real map of the Phoenix metro. Leaflet 1.9.4 (BSD-2) is stored in `leaflet/`; the tiles are the free, keyless **OpenStreetMap** standard tiles, tinted navy/blue in CSS (deep navy at night). CARTO's free basemaps also load without a key, but their free terms cover non-commercial use only and this is a business pitch, so the OSM tiles are used (light use, never pre-cached). The attribution (© OpenStreetMap contributors, plus ADOT / ODbL when those layers are on) is always visible bottom-left. The 3D 🗺 district and the 3D stand are unchanged.
- **Pan + pinch-zoom** always work. **✋ on** is needed to move a pin (drag it) or to drop one: tap the map → a 🎯 candidate pin with its site score → "＋ 🏪 New shop here". ➕ ➖ ⌂ work too.
- **Pins**: every shop has a lat/lng. The pin shows the current stage icon (☕ when open, ❌ when REC-rejected) inside a 🟢/🟠/🔴 status ring (red ones pulse), with the city name under it (names that would overlap are hidden until you zoom in). The 11 examples sit at real intersections that match their names: Rural Rd & Broadway (Tempe), Main St & Dobson (Mesa), 44th St by Sky Harbor (Phoenix), Saguaro Blvd (Fountain Hills), Lake Pleasant Pkwy & Happy Valley (Peoria), Val Vista & Warner (Gilbert), Scottsdale Rd & Indian School (Scottsdale), Arizona Ave by Loop 202 (Chandler), Estrella Pkwy & McDowell (Goodyear), Westgate (Glendale) and Bell & Litchfield (Surprise).
- **Pin card** (bottom in portrait, left in landscape, never over the map centre or the attribution): name, stage + %, the top issue (or the next / late task), 🧊 3D (flies into that shop's 3D stand) and 📊 the site score.
- **📊 Site score 0–100**, with every part shown: 🚦 traffic 40 (the busiest counted road within ¼ mile, ADOT 2024 AADT, from 5k → 0 to 60k+ cars/day → 40 on a log scale; freeways count ×0.4 because there is no driveway onto a freeway), 🏁 competitors 20 (coffee competitors from the OSM snapshot within 1 mile: a drive-thru counts 1, a walk-in coffee brand ½; 0 → 20 pts, 6 or more → 0), ☕ cannibalization 20 (nearest existing Dutch Bros or other shop in this tracker: under 1 mile → 0, 3+ miles → 20), 🛣 corner lot 10 and 🚗 stacking room for 20+ cars 10 (manual checkboxes, saved per shop). A part with no data is marked and counted as neutral (half points).
- **🗂 Layers** (toggles, saved): 🚦 **Traffic** = real ADOT 2024 Average Annual Daily Traffic (`traffic-aadt-2024.json`: real data, verified against the live layer. 4,212 road segments with 8k+ cars/day in the metro, fetched 2026-10-11 from ADOT MPD's "ADOT 2024 Average Annual Daily Traffic (AADT)" feature service on AZGeo (ArcGIS item 269b42334ec94589a1c1835ba4d15a3e, 2024 HPMS submittal). It is public ADOT open data with no licence restrictions stated, provided "as is" with no warranty; the source and terms are written in the file); ☕ **Existing Dutch Bros** (`existing-dutch-bros.json`) and 🏁 **Competitors** (`competitors.json`: `amenity=cafe` + `drive_through=yes`, or Starbucks, Black Rock, Scooter's, Human Bean, Dunkin', Biggby, 7 Brew, Ziggi's), both from one OpenStreetMap snapshot, © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL. **Snapshot:** OpenStreetMap data as of 2026-10-11 21:36 UTC (Overpass base timestamp), fetched with `osm-snapshot.mjs` through the overpass.openstreetmap.fr mirror (overpass-api.de was unreachable from the build machine): **51 existing Dutch Bros** and **438 competitors** (100 tagged drive-thru; 298 Starbucks, 66 Dunkin', 38 Black Rock, 10 Scooter's, 6 Human Bean, 3 Ziggi's, …). OSM coverage is volunteer-made, so some shops may be missing or out of date; the layer shows the snapshot date. To refresh: `node osm-snapshot.mjs` in this folder (node 18+, `OVERPASS=<mirror url>` optional). It sends one request and rewrites both files. **🔑 Legend**: ring colours, icons, the traffic colour ramp and the score weights. Both panels are collapsible and stay in a corner.
- **Filters**: the same 🏪 All / 🏗 Opening / ☕ Open / ⚠️ Attention chips dim the other pins.
- **Without tiles** (offline, blocked, or the tile server is down) the map keeps a plain background, every pin stays in place and a small "🗺 Tiles offline · pins OK" chip shows. Tiles are never pre-cached (OSM tile policy); only Leaflet and the JSON layers are in the offline cache.
- **Saves**: `coffee-opening-v4`. v1–v3 saves are migrated (the old keys stay as a backup): examples go to their intersection; your own shops without a lat/lng get a spot near downtown Phoenix, spread out on a spiral so none overlap; nothing else changes. ⬇/⬆ JSON carries `ll` (lat/lng) and the site checks; older exports import through the same migration.
- **Update fix**: GitHub Pages sends `max-age=600`, so the old sw's `fetch()` could be answered from the browser's HTTP cache and keep showing the previous version after a deploy. Now navigations and app files (html/js/json/manifest) are fetched with `cache: 'no-cache'` (network first, cache fallback offline), install bypasses the HTTP cache, and `sw.js` is registered with `updateViaCache: 'none'`. When a new version takes over (`controllerchange`), or the live `sw.js` names a newer cache than the page, the page reloads **once** and shows a small "⬆ Updated" toast in the corner (guarded so it can never loop). The offline fallback is kept.

## Progress rules
- **Stage %** = ticked tasks ÷ tasks. For 🗳, the vote counts as one more item, done only when it is ✅ Approved. For 📅 One month out, "stage 7 at 100%" and "stage 8 at 100%" count as 2 more items (it is a readiness rollup).
- **❌ Rejected** stops stages 5–11: they show 0%, red ⛔, 🔴 Blocked, and the building stays empty.
- **Status** is automatic: ✅ done, 🟠 at risk (a task or the stage is past its date, or the vote is ⏸ Deferred), 🔴 blocked (rejected), 🟢 on track otherwise. You can override it per stage (🤖 auto / 🟢 / 🟠 / 🔴).
- **Overall %** (top bar) = the average of the 11 stages. **🎉 N** = days to the grand-opening date. Tap it to change the date.

## Using it
- **Tap** a station, its floating label, the building, or a timeline node. The camera glides there and the panel opens: a bottom sheet on a phone held upright (at most about half the scene), a floating side panel in landscape. The panel shows %, status, 👤 owner, 📅 due date, the vote buttons or the readiness checks, and the checklist.
- **Checklist**: tap the box to tick. ✏️ renames, 📅 sets a date (red when overdue), 🗑 deletes (tap twice). ＋ adds a task. ◀ ▶ step through the stages.
- **📋** lists all 11 stages with bars (on an open store it opens the dashboard). **🏪 ▾** is the store picker (see above).
- **🎬 Build tour** (on a store; on the 🗺 map 🎬 is the district tour): plays the whole build from 0 to 100% in 60 s, with the camera following each stage. It never changes your data. ⏹ or any timeline tap stops it.
- **🌙 / ☀️**: night mode (deep navy, light-blue neon routes, lit lamps and windows) or day mode (light, navy and royal-blue accents).
- **✋ Drag** is off by default, so swipes and pinches on the scene do nothing. Switch it on (orange outline) to drag-pan, pinch-zoom, twist-rotate or wheel-zoom. ➕ ➖ ⟳ ⌂ always work.
- **Saving**: every edit is saved to `localStorage` (`coffee-opening-v5`). ⚙ → ⬇ Export JSON / ⬆ Import JSON (it accepts a full export or one store; anything else is rejected and your data is kept).

## Tech
- Plain files, no build step. `three.module.min.js` is three.js r169 (MIT), vendored so the app works offline.
- `data.js`: stages, the example plan, progress, status, load and import. It has no DOM, so it also runs in node.
- `app.js`: the Three.js scene (one `lot` group for the store and one `dist` group for the 🗺 map; the issue bubbles are emoji canvas sprites) (OrthographicCamera, flat-shaded low-poly boxes and cylinders, ground progress rings, dotted routes with glowing pulses, movers), the HTML labels, the panel, the timeline, the tour, and input.
- Phones: the pixel ratio is capped at 2, there is one shadow-casting light (1024 map), and geometry stays low (about 17k triangles). The dots on each route are a single instanced mesh.
- `sw.js`: cache `coffee-opening-v5`, network first that really goes to the network (see the update fix), with a cache fallback. On activate it deletes only keys starting with `coffee-opening-` (the origin is shared with the other games). Map tiles are cross-origin and never cached.
- `leaflet/`: Leaflet 1.9.4 (`leaflet.js`, `leaflet.css`, BSD-2 `LICENSE`), loaded only when 📍 opens. `traffic-aadt-2024.json` (ADOT), `existing-dutch-bros.json` + `competitors.json` (OSM snapshot, ODbL), `osm-snapshot.mjs` (the small snapshot tool: refreshes the two OSM files with one Overpass request; not loaded by the app or cached). `window.CO_TILES` can override the tile URL (self-hosting or tests).
