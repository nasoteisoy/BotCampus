Space Battles 3D

Tap your green planet, then a linked planet to send half its ships + troops. Drag to orbit, pinch to zoom.

  1. ⭐ Admiral (⚙30): ships only move with one ⚓ — it flies with the fleet. You start with one.

🎖 Generals (⚙30) guard a planet. Losing a planet loses its town. The red AI plays by the same rules.
