Space Battles 3D

Owning a planet builds nothing by itself. A 🚀 Shipyard builds spaceships (watch them orbit). A 🪖 Barracks trains land troops (little tanks on the ground).

Tap your green-glow planet, then a linked planet to send half its fleet — it carries half the troops too (needs an admiral ⭐).

Ships win the fight in space, then the landed troops fight the troops on the ground. No troops, no capture. Neutral planets have troops guarding them.

Spend ⚙ resources to build: Shipyard, Barracks, Factory (shipyards and barracks work faster) or Defense (turrets). Tap your selected planet again to build a shipyard.

Admirals ⭐ lead the next fleet sent from their planet as a glowing flagship. If that fleet loses, the admiral escapes to your nearest free planet (lost only if none). Generals 🎖 build a bunker and guard their planet — lost if it falls. Tap a portrait chip to see its perk.

⚓ No admiral, no movement. Ships can only leave a planet that has an admiral, and the admiral flies with the fleet. Planets marked ⚓ are stranded: their ships still defend, but can't move until you hire (⭐) or bring an admiral there. You start with one.

Conquer every red-glow planet. Drag to orbit, pinch to zoom.
