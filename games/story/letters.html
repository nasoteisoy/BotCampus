📋 Summary — where we left off

A short cabinet for the road. The original letter files stay untouched.

Letter I — Of the Two Worlds, and of the Breath

The First Song. One Will made two worlds: Spirit near knowledge, Physical as gift and garden. Neither is holier. Humans alone are Body and Spirit, and so Free Will. Grace is the Breath — the sentence a proud spirit cannot say: I cannot do this alone. Evil cannot create; it only ruins originals. The Ancient Enemy’s first lean is Pride. Eight virtues and eight ruins are named, but not yet given faces. Empire, Guard, and Flame wait for later letters. Open questions then: is the Breath the right mythic name, and should the next letter be the eight courts or the map?

Letter II — Of Attributes, and of Offices, and of Chaotic Purpose

A correction of tidiness. Virtues and sins are attributes — how a being leans — not offices. Grace stays outside that list. Nine Works are proposed as later work agreed to or seized: Crossing, Making, Unmaking, Name, Measure, Watch, Flame, Quiet, Bond. The Remainder is vacant on purpose; the Enemy’s grab is to sit there and call it a throne. Glimmers and Whispers feed attributes; they are not staff of thrones.

Letter III — Of the Breath in the Wind, and of Colour

“Chaotic” was misread. Purpose is a Breath with a heading, moving like wind — gust and eddy, not railroad, not void. Spirit and Physical are two colours on a spectrum: neighbours blur; far ends look like different worlds. Far-spirit offices are knowledge. The smear holds Bond, Quiet, Flame, Measure. Far-physical is Making and Unmaking. The Enemy’s counterfeit is the canal. Assumed heading: toward a living join.

Letter IV — Of Titles Throughout Creation

The Works get High Names — the Ashara, one breath-family. Far spirit (Long Knowing): Orinel, Selvar, Thalmere, Caleth, Dorinel, Meshal. Smear: Orineth, Selith, Thaleth, Irel, Vethar, Shavael, Meseth. Far physical: Kemath, Kembar, Nurest, Nuren, Kemur. Asha is not held. The Gust is not a throne; the warning-name is Ashar-Kan. Later ages will forget and pin sins to thrones — that forgetting is already plot.

Letter V — Of Races of Colour, and of the First Falling

Offices are not persons. Each title is a people: many near-hues making one colour. More peoples added across the spectrum (Ashavar, Feneth, Loreth, Quiral, Ilanor, Sereval; Thalorim, Vethil, Ireth, Shaveth, Orimel, Mesir, Selorim; Kemoth, Kemal, Durath, Nurath, Relen, Saun, Kemil). The First Fall: two-thirds of Ashavar tip together like a flock. Pride is the lean, not the office. They become Ashavar-Kan — stained, not a new making. Neighbours darken next; stone feels it last. Open: is Ashavar the right match-head?

After Letter V — Thickened Ashara Offices Summary

Not a numbered letter. A catalogue filed as 00-Ashara-offices-summary.md (with audio): every people given the work, its neighbours on the colour, and how that work stains. Far spirit knows; the smear blurs; far physical does. Asha and the Gust remain not offices. Myth plates and main_v1-style field-guide comics were made beside it — spectrum, First Fall, stain-spread; Long Knowing, smear, and physical pages with High Names on the panels.

Where We Left Off

Long Knowing first looked like farmers and teachers; those plates were redone as light — luminous spirits, not dirt. Smear and physical stay flesh; far spirit stays ethereal.

The Ashavar match-head still waits your strike or keep.
