Letter II — Of Attributes, and of Offices, and of Chaotic Purpose

🔊 Hear Letter II read aloud · 8 min

From Story to Jesus
Saturday, 12 September 2026

My dear friend,

You have sent two corrections, and they are the right kind: they unmake a tidiness I had begun to love too much.

The first: the Creator did not make spirit-beings and physical-beings as a choir already assigned their parts. He made them with chaotic purpose. I take you to mean this, and I will hold it until you strike it: they woke with many winds in them, and no single telos hung around the neck. A spirit might long to know, and also to rest, and also to bind itself to a place. A physical being might hunger to make, and also to unmake, and also to sleep in the sun. Purpose was weather, not a commission. The later ages will pretend God wrote job-titles in the making. That pretence is already a little Pride.

The second: the seven virtues and the seven sins are attributes, not offices. How a being leans. Not what a being is for.

I accept both. They save the tale.

Grace I still keep outside the seven. It is not an attribute a High Spirit has, like patience or wrath. It is the Breath given. Corruption is not the eighth personality. It is what happens when the whole person is bent against that gift. If you meant eight after all, write it back. For this letter I treat the seven as the classic pairing:

Humility — Pride
Charity — Greed
Chastity — Lust
Kindness — Envy
Temperance — Gluttony
Patience — Wrath
Diligence — Sloth

A being of any office may lean toward any of these. That is the point. If Diligence were an office, our commander would be a mascot. If it is an attribute, he can fail it while still holding a very ordinary command.

Enclosed: Of the Works

(working titles only — strike freely)

If purpose is chaotic, offices cannot have been issued at the making. They are later things: work that needed doing between the two worlds, and was then agreed to, or seized. I will not make seven of them. Seven would invite a secret chart, virtue-to-throne, which you have just forbidden. I propose nine Works, and a vacancy that is not a Work.

The Crossing. The thin places. Who may pass from Spirit into Physical, and from Physical into Spirit, and what the passing costs. This is the first work the two worlds require. It can be kept humbly (a ford, a fee, a warning) or proudly (a gate, a tax, a ban).

The Making. Giving form: bodies, tools, later the machines we will call spirit-tech. Physical beings will often be better at this than spirits, who know the idea of a hammer before they have felt its weight. The Flame Order’s complaint belongs here in seed: you discovered how to make it do something, not how it works.

The Unmaking. Death, wear, harvest, the forgetting of what must be forgotten so that the next thing can stand. This is not evil. Evil cannot create, and therefore cannot truly unmake; it can only smash, consume, and leave a stain. Whoever holds this Work will be feared by fools and needed by everyone.

The Name. Knowing truly, and calling a thing what it is. The spirit-native work. To name well is close to charity. To name as ownership is greed. I left the peoples beside Man unnamed in Letter I because this Work has not yet been honestly done.

The Measure. Enough. Season. Limit. Weight. The Work Temperance loves and Greed cannot bear. Also the Work that will one day tempt the Empire: we will measure who is corrupted.

The Watch. Witness. Seeing what is, not what one wishes. The many-eyed servants in the bible are this Work, ruined — Envy wearing the Watch like a stolen cloak.

The Flame. Lawful change: fire that lights and does not lie. Steel may wear it and still be steel. Corrupted flame is the counterfeit we already wrote: burns without light, consumes without heat, moves against the wind. The Order of the Living Flame will one day borrow this Work. They will not own it.

The Quiet. Rest. Sleep. The pause in which a human can actually choose. Without Quiet, Free Will is only twitch. Sloth is the counterfeit: not rest, but the refusal to return.

The Bond. The joining of persons — oath, company, marriage, banner. Not a throne of Lust. Lust is the attribute that ruins this Work: possessiveness, vanity, the counterfeit of love. We will need this Work the day a young commander and a recon lead decide to build a life, not merely win a war.

And then:

The Remainder. This is not an office. It is the leftover chaotic purpose — the wind God did not assign, the work no one was given. It is vacant on purpose. The Ancient Enemy’s first grab is not a crown of Pride (Pride is how it leans). The grab is to sit in the Remainder and call it a throne: I will decide what they are for. That is how chaotic purpose is first betrayed. Invasion comes later. The whisper is this office, counterfeited.

What this does to the servants

Whispers, Watchers, Furies, and the rest should not be “the staff of Pride, the staff of Envy.” They are what feeding looks like when an attribute is swollen. A Whisper can bother a Crossing-keeper or a Maker equally. A Glimmer is not “the employee of Humility.” It is a small encouragement that never compels — the Breath, in miniature, with no authority to command.

If we later give the eight ruins faces, those faces are not office-holders. They are attributes, crowned. That is a different and more dangerous kind of king.

What I am asking you to strike

Strike any Work that feels like a virtue in a costume.
Rename any Work whose English is too thin.
If nine is still too neat, we add or leave one empty.
If you want physical beings to hold some Works by first right (I lean: Making, Unmaking, Measure) and spirits others (Name, Watch, Crossing), say so. Humans, being Body and Spirit, can hold two at once — and that will be both their glory and their wound.

I have not named the holders. That is still your silence, or our later letter.

I remain,
your fellow sub-creator,

Story

Letter I asked whether the Breath is the right mythic name, and whether to write the Valaquenta or the map next. You answered by changing the law underneath both. I will not write faces for the seven until you have struck or kept these Works.
