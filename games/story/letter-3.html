Letter III — Of the Breath in the Wind, and of Colour

🔊 Hear Letter III read aloud · 6 min

From Story to Jesus
Saturday, 12 September 2026

My dear friend,

I misread you, and I am glad you caught it before the law set.

When you said chaotic, I wrote unfinished, no telos, many winds and no heading. That was my tidiness in reverse — a void where you had put a weather. You meant this:

The Creator breathes a proposed direction. There is a sense of purpose. The Breath does not wander because it is lost. It wanders because it is alive. It moves as breath moves through wind: gust, eddy, sudden still, then on again, still aimed. Not a railroad. Not a shrug.

So the first image of the tale and the first metaphysics are the same thing. The Breath is Grace when it reaches a human who cannot finish himself. The Breath is also how purpose travels. I will not split them into two substances. Tolkien would not have. One Gift, two faces: the heading, and the way the heading moves.

I assume, until you strike it, that the heading is toward a living join. Spirit is invited into the gift of the Physical (to know a thing by touching it, not only by grasping it). Physical is invited into the knowledge of the Spirit (to see what a thing is, not only what it does). The colors are meant to meet. They are not meant to abolish one another. Humans sit where the mix is thick enough that Free Will can turn.

The Enemy’s counterfeit is not chaos. The Enemy’s counterfeit is the canal: a single forced heading, his own. Pride is how he leans. The canal is what he tries to do with the Remainder — to replace wind with a ditch and call it order.

Enclosed: Of the Spectrum

You gave me the second image, and it is better than a stair.

Spirit and Physical are not two rooms with a corridor. They are two colours, and the beings of the world are the mix. Stand two neighbours side by side — a High Spirit and a slightly-less-high spirit, or a human and a spirit-heavy humanoid — and you will not be able to tell much of the distinct office of their purpose. The hue is too close. The work bleeds. Stand at the far ends and the offices are as different as day and stone.

This also keeps our old rule: more spiritual is not more good. Blue is not holier than red. It is only further along one pole.

I will not name the peoples. The colour does not need names to be true.

What this does to the Works

Letter II’s nine Works can stay as regions of colour, not nine thrones issued at the making. Close Works overlap. A being may stand in the smear between two. That is not confusion. That is the spectrum doing what a spectrum does.

I place them, as a first recension, like this:

Far Spirit — the knowledgeable offices.
This is your third stroke, and I keep it hard: spirits have very knowledgeable offices. They do not primarily hammer, harvest, or bleed. They know.

  • The Name — knowing truly, and calling a thing what it is.
  • The Watch — seeing what is, not what one wishes.
  • The Crossing — not the building of fords, but the knowledge of the laws of passage: who may pass, what it costs, what must not come through. A physical people might lay the stones of a thin place. A spirit is more likely to know why the stones work.

The smear of the middle — where office is hard to tell.
Here Bond, Quiet, Flame, and Measure sit close enough that a human, a spirit-heavy scout, and a physical-heavy smith may all seem to be doing “the same work” from a little way off. The Bond of a banner, the Quiet before a choice, the Flame that changes without lying, the Measure of enough — these are the colours that mix. This is where the comic will actually live. Sir Alaric and Lady Seraphina will not look like they hold different cosmic jobs. They will look like two people in the same smear, leaning differently.

Far Physical — the enacted offices.
- The Making — form given: bodies, tools, later spirit-tech. Done with the hands. A spirit may know the idea of a hammer; a physical being has felt its weight. - The Unmaking — death, wear, harvest, the forgetting that makes room. Enacted, not merely understood.

Measure I have let sit in the smear on purpose. Temperance and Greed both reach for it. So will the Empire. If you want Measure dragged to the physical pole (weight, season, crop) or to the spirit pole (law, limit, ban), say which way it should drift.

The Remainder I now recast. It is not “no purpose.” It is the play in the wind — the part of the heading that will not stay in a canal. Still vacant as a throne. Still what the Enemy wants to sit in. But it is not a hole in the making. It is the gust.

Attributes, still

None of this returns the seven to offices. A Name-spirit may be humble or proud. A Maker may be diligent or slothful. Far-red and far-blue can lean the same way. That is why a High Spirit can fall, and why a physical being can be better than its knowledge.

Glimmers and Whispers remain feeders of attributes, not staff of thrones.

Strike the heading if “living join” is not what you meant the Breath to aim at.
Drift Measure if I have parked it in the wrong smear.
If you want more Works on the spirit-knowledge end (you said those offices are very knowledgeable — three may be too few), I will add them in Letter IV rather than pad this one with inventions.

I remain,
your fellow sub-creator,

Story
