Letter V — Of Races of Colour, and of the First Falling

🔊 Hear Letter V read aloud · 10 min

From Story to Jesus
Saturday, 12 September 2026

My dear friend,

You have unmade the last secret throne.

The Ashara are not persons. They are not Valar with names and spouses. Each title is a people: many small alike colours, standing so near one another that from a little way off they are one hue. Orinel is not a knower. Orinel is a race of knowing. Kemath is not a smith-god. Kemath is a people who have split their palms on the first hammer, each a slightly different brown.

This is your spectrum kept honest. A colour is never one drop. It is a crowd of drops that agree almost.

A single being may lean proud or humble. A people can tip. That is the new law, and it is the one the First Falling needs.

Enclosed: More of the Ashara

I keep the titles already given. I add. Same breath-family. Same rule: close names share roots; far names should feel like different weather. Strike any sound.

Far Spirit — more of the Long Knowing

Ashavar (Heading-Knowledge). The people who perceive the proposed direction of Asha — not the Breath itself, which no race holds, but the lean of the wind. They know there is a heading. They do not are the heading. I set them at the deepest blue I dare. They will matter in a moment.

Feneth (Inward Knowing). The knowledge of what is hidden in a will: motive, wound, the prayer not yet spoken. Neighbour to Caleth and Selvar. Ruined, Feneth becomes a Whisper with a library.

Loreth (Between-Speech). The knowing of how the two worlds talk — omen, resonance, the sentence that is true in both colours at once. Neighbour to Orinel and Thalmere.

Quiral (the Owing). The knowing of what is owed: gift, debt, the unforced return. Neighbour to Meshal. The later Church-of-the-heart will almost call this justice. Greed and envy both try to wear it.

Ilanor (the Knowing that Stops). The knowledge of when not to know. The limit inside intellect. Neighbour to Meshal and Shavael’s far-blue cousin. A race that can close a book. Rare, and easy to call cowardice.

Sereval (Pattern). The knowing of how one motion will breed another — not prophecy as doom, but the grain of the wood. Neighbour to Caleth and Dorinel.

The Smear — more mixed work

Thalorim (Ushers). Neither Passage-Law nor Ford-Laying. The people who walk with a crossing: guides, ferrymen of the thin places. Between Thalmere and Thaleth, and from a little way off they are both.

Vethil (the Near Bond). Household, pair, the two-person oath. Vethar’s smaller sister. Marriage will sit here long before anyone speaks of banners.

Ireth (Hearth-Flame). Irel stepped nearer the hands: cookfire, lamp, the small lawful change that lets a camp live. The Flame Order will one day look at Ireth and not notice they have walked past Irel.

Shaveth (Sleep). Shavael’s neighbour: not the pause of choice, but the body’s nightly unmaking-and-return. Dreams live on this smear. Sloth counterfeits it; so does the Enemy, with sleeps that do not rest.

Orimel (Working Names). Nicknames, trail-marks, the true-enough name a banner uses in the rain. Orinel’s grandchild. Comic dialogue will live here and never say so.

Mesir (the Shared Enough). Not law, not ration: the moment a company agrees we have what we need. Neighbour to Vethar and Meseth. Charity leans here without owning the seat.

Selorim (the Second Look). Selith’s twin-hue — not the first watch, but the one who doubts the first watch. Recon’s Mute and Speaker will one day stand in Selith and Selorim without a High Word for either.

Far Physical — more enacted peoples

Kemoth (Green Making). Cultivation, growth, the seed that is a making you wait for. Neighbour to Kemath and Nuren.

Kemal (Living Making). The making of bodies from bodies — birth, herd, the child. Not Lust’s throne. Lust is the attribute that can claim a person like a tool. Kemal is the work of life continuing. Keep it modest, always.

Durath (Slow Stone). Mountains, walls that think in centuries, the making so slow it looks like being. Neighbour to Kemur and Nurest.

Nurath (Sky-Wear). Weather, tide of air, the unmaking that comes from above. Neighbour to Nuren.

Relen (the Moving Herd). Beasts, migration, the enacted purpose that does not lecture and does not need to. A far-red people. From the spirit pole they look like weather with legs.

Saun (Water’s Double Work). Making and unmaking in one motion: river, blood, rain. Sits on the last smear before far-physical. Thaleth’s wet cousin.

Kemil (Mending). The making that goes back into a wound. Neighbour to Kemur and Nurest. Patience loves this people. Wrath cannot stand them.

Enclosed: Of the First Falling

I take your numbers as law until you change them.

One office-race falls first. Two-thirds of that people fall together. Not a lonely prince in a dark hall. A colour tipping. The remaining third do not become a different race; they become a remnant of the same hue, now terribly awake.

I assume the first people are Ashavar.

They were made to know the heading of the Breath — the proposed direction, the sense of purpose inside the wind. That is a knowledgeable office, far blue, close to Orinel, Dorinel, Caleth, and Meshal. Their temptation is almost the work itself: if we know the heading, we can be the heading.

One among them leans first. (The bible’s single High Spirit I keep as the first lean, not as the whole office.) Pride is the attribute. The office is not Pride. Then two-thirds of Ashavar go with that lean, in one motion, as a flock turns. That is your chain’s match-head.

What they become is not a new making. Evil cannot create. They are still Ashavar, stained: Ashavar-Kan, the canal-people, who will later be mis-titled Ashar-Kan as if one throne sat in the Gust. We should let later ages make that mistake. The truth is a majority of a colour.

Why two-thirds starts a chain

Because a colour is made of neighbours.

When two-thirds of Ashavar tip, the remnant cannot hold the hue alone. The colour of Heading-Knowledge darkens. The next-nearest peoples — Dorinel, Meshal, Caleth, Sereval, Orinel — begin to see a sky that has already changed. Some of their drops stain. Not two-thirds, not at first. A smear of a smear. That is enough.

Dorinel (Distance) starts to rank the spectrum: farther blue means holier.
Meshal (Enough-as-Law) starts to legislate the ranking.
Selvar starts to watch for those who will not be ranked.
Orinel starts to name the ranks as if they were truths.
Feneth starts to hunt inward for dissent.

Then the smear feels it as weather, not as argument: Thalmere’s law of passage grows a tax; Thaleth’s fords grow gates; Irel is canalised into flame that burns without light; Vethar starts to mean possession; Shavael is refused, or is never left.

Far physical feels it last and least as idea. Kemath and Nurest do not understand the canal. They feel a wrong weight in the hammer, a harvest that does not feed. Relen’s herds startle. Saun runs against the season. That is how the Physical World learns the Fall: not by lecture. By stain.

No race is wholly lost except the two-thirds of Ashavar who chose together. Even they are not a new species. They are a ruined original. The remnant third of Ashavar become the first people who know, in their bones, the sentence the proud spirit cannot say: we cannot be the Breath.

I have not named the first leaner. That silence is still yours.

If Ashavar is the wrong match-head — if you would rather the first fall be Dorinel (ranking), or Orinel (naming-as-claim), or Meshal (law) — strike it and I will rewrite the chain. The shape I will keep: a people, two-thirds, neighbours next, stone last.

I remain,
your fellow sub-creator,
