The Ashara — offices, thickened

After Letter V: a catalogue of every people of the Ashara — their work, their neighbours on the colour, and how that work stains.

🔊 Hear the Ashara summary · 16 min

Ashara plates

The Ashara are peoples, not gods. Many near-hues make one colour. A single being may be proud or humble. A people can tip. Virtues and sins are how someone leans, not a job title. Asha, the Breath, is gift and heading. No race sits in it.

Far spirit — they know

Here office is almost the same thing as knowledge. They do not hammer. They know, and the knowing is the work.

Ashavar — Heading-Knowledge.
They perceive the proposed direction of the Breath: that the wind has a heading, not that they are the heading. Deepest blue we have dared. Neighbours: Orinel, Dorinel, Caleth, Meshal, Sereval. Their temptation is the work itself — if we know the heading, we can be the heading. One leaned first. Two-thirds of the people turned with that lean, like a flock, and became Ashavar-Kan, the canal-people: still Ashavar, stained, not a new making. The remnant third stay Ashavar, and are the first who know in their bones that they cannot be the Breath. Pride was how they leaned. The office was never Pride.

Orinel — True-Name.
They know a thing as it is, and can call it without owning it. To speak Orinel honestly is charity’s cousin. The heresy is to name as a claim. After the Fall, stained Orinel start naming ranks as if ranks were truths. Neighbours: Caleth, Loreth, Ashavar. Down-spectrum they blur into Orineth (the mouth-name of a trail). Mortals who “have a way with the true word” are standing in this smear without the High Title.

Selvar — Clear Sight.
They see what stands, not what would be convenient. A wished-illusion may appear; they let it fade unused. Ruined, this knowledge becomes the many-eyed hunger (Watchers). After the Fall they begin to watch for those who will not be ranked. Neighbours: Feneth, Dorinel, Selith. From a little way off, Selvar and Dorinel look like the same blue.

Thalmere — Passage-Law.
They know the thin places: who may pass, what the passing costs, what must never come through. They do not lay stones. A mason may build a ford and never hold Thalmere. After the Fall the law of passage grows a tax. Neighbours: Loreth, Thaleth, Thalorim. Two colours, one ford: Thalmere knows why; Thaleth makes it walkable.

Caleth — First Motions.
They know how a thing begins: the first lean of a will, the first crack in a peace, the first honest prayer. This is why a High Spirit can understand courage as a theorem before any human has had to choose it. They do not plant seeds; beginnings of will are their field. Neighbours: Ashavar, Sereval, Feneth. After the Fall, some Caleth start causing first leans instead of knowing them.

Dorinel — Distance.
They know where a being sits on the colour — how far from stone, how far from light. The office that keeps the spectrum from collapsing into “higher means holier.” After two-thirds of Ashavar tip, Dorinel is among the first to stain: they start to rank the spectrum. Neighbours: Selvar, Sereval, Ashavar. Difference without rank is the clean work. Rank is the stain.

Meshal — Enough-as-Law.
Measure on the spirit pole: limit, ban, season-of-the-soul. The stop that is a law, not a ration. The Empire will one day counterfeit Meshal and call it a census of the corrupted. After the Fall, stained Meshal legislate Dorinel’s new ranking. Neighbours: Quiral, Ilanor, Meseth. A priest of limits and a quartermaster will each think the other is doing their job wrong.

Feneth — Inward Knowing.
They know what is hidden in a will: motive, wound, the prayer not yet spoken. Neighbour to Caleth and Selvar. Clean Feneth can show a person their own wound without using it. Ruined Feneth becomes a Whisper with a library — inward sight as leverage.

Loreth — Between-Speech.
They know how the two worlds talk: omen, resonance, the sentence that is true in both colours at once. Neighbour to Orinel and Thalmere. A later age will call this prophecy and get it wrong. Clean Loreth translate. Stained Loreth make the two worlds lie in one mouth.

Quiral — the Owing.
They know what is owed: gift, debt, the unforced return. Neighbour to Meshal. The later Church-of-the-heart will almost call this justice. Greed wants to collect. Envy wants to see another charged. Charity is the lean that leaves Quiral clean.

Ilanor — the Knowing that Stops.
They know when not to know. The limit inside intellect. A people who can close the book. Neighbour to Meshal and to Shavael’s far-blue cousin. Rare. Easy to call cowardice. After the Fall, some Ilanor close the book on mercy, and some refuse to close it on secrets that should stay shut.

Sereval — Pattern.
They know how one motion will breed another — not doom-prophecy, the grain of the wood. Neighbour to Caleth and Dorinel. Clean Sereval can say “this road tends toward that weather.” Stained Sereval make people believe the pattern is a canal: no choice, only track.

The smear — hard to tell apart

Here the Breath is loudest as wind. A human, a spirit-heavy scout, and a physical-heavy wright may all seem, from a little way off, to be doing the same work.

Orineth — Called Name.
Orinel stepped nearer the mouth. They find the true-enough name of a route or a fear, not the High Name of a being. Translators, trail-readers. Recon will live here without knowing the title. Neighbours: Orinel, Orimel, Selith. They will argue for centuries which of them saw first and which of them named it.

Orimel — Working Names.
Nicknames, trail-marks, the true-enough name a banner uses in the rain. Orinel’s grandchild. Comic dialogue lives here and will never say so. Neighbour to Orineth and Vethar. A working name can save a life. A working name can also shrink a person to a joke.

Selith — Near Watch.
Sentries, scouts, the one who looks twice — at a ridge, a camp, a thin place. Selvar’s cousin, nearer the eyes of flesh. Neighbours: Orineth, Selorim. Clean Selith keeps people alive. Stained Selith never stops looking, and starts looking for.

Selorim — Second Look.
Not the first watch: the one who doubts the first watch. Mute and Speaker will one day stand in Selith and Selorim without a High Word for either. Neighbour to Selith. Necessary. Easy to become the person who believes nothing.

Thaleth — Ford-Laying.
Hands that mark a thin place so a body can use it. Stones, signs, a walkable crossing. Thalmere knows why; Thaleth makes the why walkable. After the Fall, fords grow gates. Neighbours: Thalmere, Thalorim, Saun.

Thalorim — Ushers.
Neither law nor masonry. They walk with a crossing: guides, ferrymen of the thin places. From a little way off they are Thalmere and Thaleth both. Clean Thalorim get you through. Stained Thalorim decide who is allowed to try.

Irel — Unlying Flame.
Lawful change. Fire that lights and does not lie. Steel may wear it and remain steel. The Order of the Living Flame will later borrow Irel. They will not own it. Corrupted flame is Irel canalised: burns without light, consumes without heat, moves against the wind. Neighbours: Ireth, Caleth.

Ireth — Hearth-Flame.
Irel stepped nearer the hands: cookfire, lamp, the small lawful change that lets a camp live. The Flame Order will look at Ireth and not notice they have walked past Irel. Neighbour to Irel, Mesir, Shaveth. A hearth can be welcome. A hearth can be a demand that everyone sit in one light.

Vethar — the Bond.
Oath, company, marriage, banner. The joining of persons. Lust does not sit here; Lust is the attribute that can rot any Vethar from the inside — possessiveness, vanity, the counterfeit of love. After the Fall, Vethar starts to mean possession. Neighbours: Vethil, Mesir.

Vethil — Near Bond.
Household, pair, the two-person oath. Vethar’s smaller sister. Marriage sits here long before anyone speaks of banners. Neighbour to Vethar and Shavael. Clean Vethil is a life built. Stained Vethil is two people who cannot let the other be a person.

Shavael — Held Pause.
The Quiet in which a human can actually choose. Not sloth. Sloth is the refusal to leave the pause. Without Shavael, Free Will is only twitch. After the Fall, Shavael is refused, or is never left. Neighbours: Shaveth, Ilanor, Vethil.

Shaveth — Sleep.
Not the pause of choice: the body’s nightly unmaking-and-return. Dreams live here. Sloth counterfeits it. The Enemy counterfeits it with sleeps that do not rest. Neighbour to Shavael, Nuren, Ireth.

Meseth — Enough-as-Felt.
Measure in the hands: ration, season, the weight that says stop. Meshal’s neighbour. Felt, not legislated. A quartermaster’s office. Greed cannot bear the stop. Gluttony cannot feel it.

Mesir — Shared Enough.
Not law, not ration: the moment a company agrees we have what we need. Neighbour to Vethar and Meseth. Charity leans here without owning the seat. Stained Mesir is a company that agrees they need more, together.

Far physical — they do

Here office is almost the same thing as doing. They may not lecture on the law. They have felt the weight. They learn the Fall last, as a wrong weight in the hammer, a harvest that does not feed, weather that will not keep the season.

Kemath — First Making.
Form given: the first tool, the first shaping of matter, the first body-form. A spirit may know the idea of a hammer. Kemath has split the palm on it. Neighbours: Kembar, Kemoth, Kemal. Clean Kemath makes a thing that can be used. Stained Kemath makes a thing that can only be owned.

Kembar — Repeated Making.
Craft-lineages. The making that learns by doing again. Spirit-tech is Kembar that has overheard Orinel and not quite understood it — the Flame Order’s old complaint, now given a people. Neighbour to Kemath and Kemur.

Kemur — Making that Wears.
Bridges, walls, armor: making that expects Unmaking and builds anyway. The smear’s last step before stone. Black Guard plate sits here, whether they know the word or not. Neighbour to Durath, Kemil, Nurest.

Kemoth — Green Making.
Cultivation. The seed that is a making you wait for. Neighbour to Kemath and Nuren. Patience loves this people. Sloth counterfeits the wait.

Kemal — Living Making.
The making of bodies from bodies: birth, herd, the child. Not Lust’s throne. Lust is the attribute that can claim a person like a tool. Kemal is life continuing. Keep it modest, always. Neighbour to Kemath, Relen, Kemil.

Kemil — Mending.
The making that goes back into a wound. Neighbour to Kemur and Nurest. Patience loves them. Wrath cannot stand them. A stained Kemil “mends” by erasing what the wound meant.

Durath — Slow Stone.
Mountains, walls that think in centuries, the making so slow it looks like being. Neighbour to Kemur and Nurest. From the spirit pole they look like weather that forgot to move.

Nurest — True Unmaking.
Death, wear, harvest, the forgetting that makes room. Not evil. Evil cannot unmake; it only smashes and stains. Neighbour to Nuren, Nurath, Kemil. Fools fear this people. Everyone needs them.

Nuren — Daily Return.
Compost, tide, the herd culled, the forest cut and left to rise. Nurest’s neighbour. From a little way off they are the same brown. The everyday of Unmaking, not the last of it.

Nurath — Sky-Wear.
Weather, tide of air, the unmaking that comes from above. Neighbour to Nuren and Relen. After the Fall the physical world feels Nurath first: season against itself.

Relen — the Moving Herd.
Beasts, migration, enacted purpose that does not lecture and does not need to. Far-red. From the spirit pole they look like weather with legs. Neighbour to Kemal, Nurath, Saun. Startled herds are how stone first hears the Fall.

Saun — Water’s Double Work.
Making and unmaking in one motion: river, blood, rain. Last smear before far-physical. Thaleth’s wet cousin. A river that runs against the banks is Saun feeling the canal.

Not offices

Asha — the Breath. Gift when it reaches a human who cannot finish himself. Heading when it moves through the world as wind. No race holds it.

The Gust — the play in the wind, the part of the heading that will not stay in a canal. Vacant as a throne. The Enemy tries to sit there and call it Ashar-Kan. Later ages will say that was one king. The truth is two-thirds of a colour.

Pride is how Ashavar leaned. The office was never Pride.
