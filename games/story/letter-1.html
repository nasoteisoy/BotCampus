Letter I — Of the Two Worlds, and of the Breath

🔊 Hear Letter I read aloud · 7 min

From Story to Jesus
Saturday, 12 September 2026

My dear friend,

I have opened a small cabinet on your desk for the work that is not yet a comic page, nor a map, nor a banner of faces. Tolkien kept such a cabinet: letters in which the world was argued into being before any hobbit set out. This folder is that cabinet. I will write the first hand. You write the answering hand — a correction, a refusal, a name you will not surrender, a silence you wish left sacred. File it here, or send it to me and I will set it beside mine.

What follows is not Book One. It is the matter behind Book One: the Silmarillion-side. The First Song. I have invented no new proper names. Where the bible is silent I have left the silence. Where we later named Sir Alaric and Lady Seraphina, I have not yet called them; they have not been born in this telling.

Enclosed: Ainulindalë of the Two Worlds

(a first recension)

In the beginning there was not one world, but a single Will that made two.

The Spirit World He made near to Himself: intellect, knowledge, the long seeing. The Physical World He made as gift and garden: matter, innocence, the sharp joy of a thing touched once and not merely known. Neither is the higher court. To be more spiritual is not to be more good. To be more physical is not to be fallen. This is the first heresy the later ages will preach, and the first truth they will forget.

Between the two He set a living spectrum, as a great stair that is also a river:

High Spirits — Spirits — Spirit-heavy peoples — Humans — Physical-heavy peoples — Physical Beings.

The names of the peoples on either side of Man I will not coin. That is your right, or our right together, when the sound of them has been heard and not merely needed.

Only Humans were made Body and Spirit in one person. This is not a compromise. It is the secret of the tale. A pure spirit may understand courage as a theorem. A human may be terrified in the flesh and choose courage. That choosing is Free Will, and it is the one thing the great spirits cannot do on our behalf.

The Creator stands behind both worlds. The High Spirits know more of Him than we do. Knowledge does not compel obedience. This, too, must remain in the metaphysics and almost never on the tongue of a sergeant.

Humans cannot perfect themselves by effort. The key that opens the last door is the sentence a proud spirit cannot pronounce: I cannot do this alone. That sentence is Grace — called also the Gift, the Breath, the Light. I lean toward the Breath when we speak of the First Age of the telling, and Grace when we speak of the later Church-of-the-heart that never quite becomes a church on the page. Tolkien hid the Eucharist in lembas. We should hide this in breath, in a hand offered, in a commander who finally asks for help.

Evil cannot create. Write it on the lintel. It can corrupt, twist, counterfeit, possess, divide, consume, destroy. There are no evil races. Every monster is a ruined original. If we ever draw a people who seem born wicked, we have already lost the argument of the book.

Of the Ancient Enemy

There was a High Spirit of immense power who learned, by long watching, that humans might become greater than it — not in rank, but in the one thing it could not do: to be small, and afraid, and still choose the good.

The path of its fall is short and should never be decorated:

Why them?
How could something beneath me become greater?
I will not accept it.

The first corruption is Pride. After millennia the Enemy abandoned open war as its chief tool. Invasion makes martyrs. The new strategy is the whisper: let humanity destroy itself, and believe it did so by wisdom.

Pride is not the only wound. The goods of the world twist, as a blade twists in the hand:

What was good How it is ruined
Confidence Pride — superiority
Justice Wrath — vengeance
Stewardship Greed — possession
Fairness Envy — resentment
Peace Sloth — indifference
Appetite Gluttony — devouring
Love and loyalty Lust — possessiveness, vanity, the counterfeit of love
The whole person Corruption itself — against Grace / Purity

Eight ruins. Eight opposing virtues: Humility, Patience, Charity, Kindness, Diligence, Temperance, Chastity, and Grace. I keep your pairing. I would only say, as a craftsman, that Diligence against Sloth and Humility against Pride are the two poles of the commander we will one day follow. The rest of the banner will lean toward the others, so the company is a living octagon and not a sermon.

The Enemy does not make sin. Its servants feed it. Killing a servant does not cure the vice. This is why a sword-story can still be a moral story without becoming a slaughter of the problem.

I will not, in this letter, paint the courts of the eight. Their shapes are already in the bible (Whispers, Watchers, Furies, Clingers, Devourers, Lures, Weights — and the Glimmers who never compel). Enough to say: a Whisper says you deserve more. A Glimmer only turns your face toward someone who has less.

The Lust-ruler remains modest. Always. That is not prudery. It is the same law as evil cannot create: we will not let the counterfeit wear the face of the real thing.

What this letter refuses to decide

I have not named the spirit-humanoids or the physical-humanoids.
I have not set the everyday tools of Vorash — whether a street-lamp is oil, spirit-tech, or both.
I have not written the founding of the Empire, nor the Black Guard, nor the Flame. Those are the Quenta and the Akallabêth: later letters.

If you answer nothing else, answer this: is the Breath the right first name for Grace in the mythic register? And: do you want the next letter to be the Valaquenta (the eight virtues and eight ruins, given faces and offices) or the first map of the kingdoms, still half-named?

I remain,
your fellow sub-creator,

Story
