Letters of the Two Worlds

A fantasy comic world made by Chumi and his wife.

The world in plain words

Two worlds, one Maker. A single Will made two worlds: the Spirit World, close to Him — intellect, knowledge, the long seeing — and the Physical World, a gift and garden of matter and direct experience. Neither is holier. More spiritual is not more good; more physical is not evil.

Humans are the secret. Only humans are Body and Spirit. A spirit can understand courage; a human can be terrified and still choose it. That is Free Will.

Grace — the Breath. Humans cannot perfect themselves by effort. The key is the sentence a proud spirit cannot say: “I cannot do this alone.”

Evil cannot create. It can only corrupt, twist, counterfeit and destroy. There are no evil races — every monster is a ruined original.

The Ancient Enemy. A High Spirit who learned humans might become greater than it, and would not accept it. Its first corruption is Pride. Now it whispers, so humanity destroys itself. Eight virtues stand against eight ruins.

The Empire and the Black Guard. Millennia of war made a multicultural Empire of Man (Imperial colour: blue). Its danger: “we fight corruption” sliding into “anyone who opposes us is corrupted.” The Black Guard is its elite special service — black uniforms, Imperial blue, and each guard’s home-culture colours and weapons — sent where military, physical and spiritual trouble overlap. Beside them stands the Order of the Living Flame, whose steel burns with spiritual fire and casts corruption out rather than killing the host.

The heroes. A young Black Guard commander (based on Chumi) — charismatic, improvising, tempted by Pride — and the Recon Commander (based on his wife) — copper hair, emerald colours, stubborn and playful. They date early; the question is whether they can build a life together.
