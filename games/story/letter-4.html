Letter IV — Of Titles Throughout Creation

🔊 Hear Letter IV read aloud · 8 min

From Story to Jesus
Saturday, 12 September 2026

My dear friend,

You asked for the creative stroke: not the Name and the Watch as school-labels, but titles — the kind a later age could swear by, fear, or forget the meaning of. Working High Names, in one breath-family so they feel like one making. The common tongue I put beside them in parentheses. Strike any sound. The law underneath stays yours: attributes are not offices; neighbouring hues blur; far spirit is knowledge; the Breath has a heading and moves like wind.

I call the whole catalogue the Ashara — the Works through which the heading of Asha (the Breath) is known or enacted. No one holds Asha. That is the Gift, not a chair.

I have not named the holders. These are seats and smears, not persons.

Enclosed: The Ashara

Far Spirit — the Long Knowing

Here office is almost indistinguishable from knowledge. They do not hammer. They know, and the knowing is the work.

Orinel (True-Name). The knowing of a thing as it is. To speak Orinel honestly is to call without owning. The first heresy in this seat is to name as a claim.

Selvar (Clear Sight). The sight that does not wish. Selvar sees what stands, not what would be convenient. Ruined, this knowledge becomes the many-eyed hunger we already wrote.

Thalmere (Passage-Law). Not the laying of stones. The knowing of the thin places: who may pass, what the passing costs, what must never come through. A later mason may build a ford and never hold Thalmere.

Caleth (First Motions). The knowing of how a thing begins — the first lean of a will, the first crack in a peace, the first honest prayer. Caleth is why a High Spirit can understand courage as a theorem before any human has had to choose it.

Dorinel (Distance). The knowing of where a being sits on the colour — how far from stone, how far from light. Dorinel is the office that keeps our spectrum from collapsing into “higher means holier.” It knows difference without ranking it as virtue.

Meshal (Enough-as-Law). Measure on the spirit pole: limit, ban, season-of-the-soul. The Empire will one day counterfeit Meshal and call it a census of the corrupted.

These six sit close. A little down-spectrum you can barely tell Orinel from Caleth, or Selvar from Dorinel. That is correct.

The Smear — where office is hard to tell

Here the Breath is loudest as wind. Titles overlap. A human, a spirit-heavy scout, and a physical-heavy wright may all seem, from a little way off, to be doing the same work.

Orineth (the Called Name). Orinel stepped nearer the mouth. Translators, trail-readers, those who find the true name of a route or a fear without being High Knowers. Recon will live in this smear without knowing the High Title.

Selith (the Near Watch). Sentries, scouts, the one who looks twice. Selvar’s cousin. Neighbours of Selith and Orineth will argue for centuries which of them saw first and which of them named it.

Thaleth (Ford-Laying). The hands that mark a thin place so a body can use it. Thalmere knows why; Thaleth makes the why walkable. Two colours, one ford.

Irel (the Unlying Flame). Lawful change. Steel may wear it and remain steel. This is the Work the Order of the Living Flame will later borrow. They will not own Irel. Corrupted flame is Irel canalised — a ditch that burns without light.

Vethar (the Bond). Oath, company, marriage, banner. The joining of persons. Lust does not sit here; Lust is an attribute that can rot any Vethar from the inside.

Shavael (the Held Pause). The Quiet in which a human can actually choose. Not sloth. Sloth is the refusal to leave the pause. Without Shavael, Free Will is only twitch.

Meseth (Enough-as-Felt). Measure in the hands: ration, season, the weight that says stop. Meshal’s neighbour. Close enough that a priest of limits and a quartermaster will each think the other is doing their job wrong.

Far Physical — the Enacted

Here office is almost indistinguishable from doing. They may not be able to lecture on the law. They have felt the weight.

Kemath (First Making). Form given: bodies, tools, the first shaping of matter. A spirit may know the idea of a hammer. Kemath has split the palm on it.

Kembar (the Repeated Making). Craft-lineages, the making that learns by doing again. Spirit-tech is Kembar that has overheard Orinel and not quite understood it — the Flame Order’s old complaint, now given a seat.

Nurest (True Unmaking). Death, wear, harvest, the forgetting that makes room. Not evil. Evil cannot unmake; it only smashes and stains.

Nuren (the Daily Return). Compost, tide, the herd culled, the forest cut and left to rise. Nurest’s neighbour. From a little way off they are the same brown.

Kemur (the Making that Wears). Bridges, walls, armor — making that expects Unmaking and builds anyway. The smear’s last step before stone. Black Guard plate will sit here, whether they know the word or not.

What is not a title

Asha is not held.
The Gust (the play in the wind, the heading that will not stay in a canal) is not a throne. The Enemy’s first grab is still this: to sit in the Gust and call it Ashar-Kan — a title I write only as a warning, not as an office we grant him.

No title above is a virtue. An Orinel may be humble or proud. A Kemath may be diligent or slothful. Far-blue and far-red can lean the same way.

The old servants (Whispers, Glimmers, and their kin) still feed attributes. They are not the staff of Orinel or Vethar.

How a later age will forget this

They will make a chart of seven, and pin a sin to a throne. They will say the High Knowers are holier. They will call Irel a god of fire and Kemath a god of the forge. That forgetting is already a plot. We should write the titles so the forgetting is possible — close enough to confuse, distinct enough that a careful reader (or a careful commander) can still tell a ford-layer from the Law of Passage.

If a title sounds like a person, change it. If two sit too far apart on the colour for their names to share a root, change one. If you want a deeper spirit-end (more Long Knowing) or a stranger physical-end (offices that are almost beasts, rivers, mountains being their work), say which direction to grow.

I remain,
your fellow sub-creator,
