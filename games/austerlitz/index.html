Field guide

🇫🇷Setup: drag a commander inside the blue area (or select him and tap a spot there). His division re-forms around him: line in front, supports, guns on good ground, horse on the flanks. ⟲ ⟳ turn it. Drag single units to fine-tune. Tapping outside the area gives his opening zone ⚑Commanders: tap one (or his button in the bottom strip), then tap a place. His division takes that zone; he picks each unit's formation, spot and facing ✏️Line: select a commander, tap ✏️ Line, draw with one finger (straight or curved). His battalions spread along it facing across it, supports behind the gaps, guns at the ends, horse behind the wings. Tap the ⇅ arrow (or ⇅ Flip) to face the other way. In setup it places him at once ⚔️Take: march up, form line against anything in the way, go in together, then dig in. 🛡️ Hold: line in front, supports behind the gaps, skirmishers ahead, guns on the flanks, horse on the wings. Each unit has its own slot (faint ghost boxes when he's selected) ▮▮March: sent any distance, a division marches as one body: columns side by side, skirmishers ahead, guns limbered behind, horse at the rear. It keeps the slowest unit's pace and waits for stragglers, files across bridges and fords (⇶) and re-forms, then deploys straight into its line. Troops move at a deliberate walk; charges are still fast 📍Tap near a village or flag to snap to it. Drag from a commander to point him. Press, hold and drag the map to draw a zone of any size. Tap an enemy: attack there ↩️Direct orders still work: tap a battalion. It rejoins its commander when the order is done, or tap ↩️ Div (🛡️ Hold stays until you do) ♞Cavalry rides in squadron pairs, guns in 2-gun sections. Pairs charging together hit like the full regiment ➜Move. ⏩ Quick: faster, no firing, tires horses ⚔️Charge: tap an enemy. Needs a run-up. Cavalry are blown 💨 afterwards and must rest 🎯Fire at an enemy or an area. Units fire on their own too 🛡️Hold: stand fast, fire up close, and form square against cavalry by itself ▬Line: full volleys, slow, weak on the flanks ▮Column: fast, best for charges, roundshot tears through it ◻Square: cavalry bounce off it, very slow, guns shred it 💥Guns must unlimber to fire (5 s) and limber to move. Roundshot bounces through deep ranks ✸Red ring: canister, huge damage up close ⛰Hill crests block sight. High ground sees farther and aims better 🌫Fog early on. Gunsmoke clouds drift with the wind and block sight 🏠Villages: best cover, block sight, hard to charge ⭐Near the Marshal, morale recovers faster and broken units rally ⁍+Ammo refills west of the Goldbach or near the Marshal ⚠Allied columns arrive from the east.
