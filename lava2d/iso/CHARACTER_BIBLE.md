# Lava Knight (iso/8) — locked character bible

Canonical playable ref: iso/ref/hero_ref.png (= idle_se). Do not redesign.

## Silhouette
- Stocky armored knight, readable at AoE2 iso scale
- Closed full helm with rising orange-yellow flame plume (not bare head)
- Broad pauldrons; heater or round shield; molten spear/sword

## Palette
- Armor: dark basalt gray / charcoal plates
- Trim: bronze / copper edges
- Lava: orange → yellow glow in chest cracks and weapon
- Plume: bright orange-yellow flame
- Always molten jagged lava sword + heater shield with rune; optional deep crimson cape (back views may show glowing back sigil)

## Signature parts (every frame)
1. Closed dark helm + orange-yellow flame plume
2. Glowing molten chest core / cracked lava breastplate
3. Shield with glowing orange rune/flame mark
4. Molten glowing spear or jagged lava blade
5. Basalt plates with bronze trim

## Flow rules
- Idle = planted stance per facing
- Walk A / Walk B = opposite-foot mid-stride continuing from that dir's idle (same gear, lean, camera)
- Neighbor dirs interpolate silhouette; no SE bias on N/NE/E labels
- W/NW/SW = horizontal flips of E/NE/SE after QC
