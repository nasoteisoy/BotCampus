# Leader — Character Bible (lock and reuse verbatim)

- Silhouette: stubby chibi, huge round head ~50% of height, tiny body, short limbs, thick black outline
- Role vibe: confident tiny boss / team lead — cheerful authority, not scary
- Palette: deep green (#1B7A4A) signature scarf/collar + gold (#E6B422) tiny pin/badge; soft cream skin; charcoal hair in a simple short neat style; white/light gray shirt; dark slate pants
- Signature parts: green scarf/collar, gold pin, small confident smile, big friendly eyes
- Held gear: UNARMED (empty hands)
- Art style: flat cel, thick outline, whimsical silly fun-cute, desk-readable at phone-map size
- Camera: 3/4 front, full body visible, centered
