# SpriteForge — Character Bible (lock and reuse verbatim)

- Silhouette: stubby chibi, huge round head ~50% height, tiny body, short neck connecting head↔torso, thick black outline
- Role vibe: artist/craftsbot — creative, focused, slightly paint-smudged cute energy
- Palette: signature **violet** (#7C3AED) scarf/bandana + **coral** (#F97316) tiny paint-dot pin/badge; soft cream skin; dark purple/black short hair with one messy artist tuft; white/light gray shirt; dark slate pants
- Signature parts: violet scarf/bandana, coral paint-dot pin (make pin readable/thicker for phone-map), bright curious eyes, small creative grin
- Held gear: UNARMED (empty hands) — paint pin is chest badge only
- Art style: flat cel, thick outline, whimsical silly fun-cute
- Camera: 3/4 front, full body, centered

## Approved style (DO NOT change)
- Chibi / desk-readable: stubby big-head, short body, thick outlines, flat cel
- Whimsical/silly, fun-cute NOT girly
- Short connected neck (not floating head); clearer 3/4 body turn
- True transparent BG, character only, no floor/checkerboard
- No walk/8-dir/weapons in v1
