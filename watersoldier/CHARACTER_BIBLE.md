# Water Soldier — locked character bible

Reuse verbatim in every frame prompt. Same character as hero_ref.png. Do not redesign.

## Silhouette
- Stocky, wide-legged heavily armored infantry (same family as Fire Soldier)
- Broad shoulders, thick pauldrons, heavy boots
- Full-face Corinthian-style helm with flowing aqua water crest plume
- Round buckler + trident/forked spear silhouette

## Palette
- Armor: deep ocean blue / teal polished plate
- Water veins: bright cyan-aqua cracks through armor plates
- Chest: large circular swirling water/pearl core (cyan with white-blue center)
- Helm visor slit: glowing cyan horizontal line
- Crest plume: flowing aqua-to-white water brush (wavy fluid — not fire, not crimson)
- Shield: ocean-stone round buckler with stylized glowing cyan WAVE emblem
- Trident: dark teal shaft; translucent glowing cyan-blue crystalline tines

## Signature parts (every frame)
1. Flowing aqua water crest plume on full-face helm
2. Glowing cyan visor slit
3. Circular swirling water chest core
4. Ocean-blue armor with cyan water vein cracks
5. Round buckler + wave emblem
6. Glowing cyan crystalline trident

## Style / camera
- Cel-shaded Clash/AoE-readable game art, hard edges, high contrast
- Isometric-friendly high camera for AoE2-style play
- True transparent background; character only; no floor; no scenery; no text
- NO cape / cloak

## Facing notes
- SE = primary hero angle (matches ref)
- Unique art: N, NE, E, SE, S for idle + walk
- W / NW / SW = horizontal flip of E / NE / SE after QC
- Attack: SE primary

## Flow rules
- Idle: feet planted, trident upright-ish, shield close to body
- Walk A: left foot forward mid-stride; trident/shield follow through from idle
- Walk B: opposite foot forward; continuous cycle with Walk A
- Neighbor dirs = small turn of SAME body, not a redesign

## Gear motion (mandatory)
- Trident angle/tips move with spear arm each frame (idle → walk A arc → walk B opposite phase)
- Round wave buckler tilts/translates with off-hand and gait — never identical transform across idle/walk A/walk B
- No frozen gear; no teleport; gear always present; no appear/disappear mid-loop
