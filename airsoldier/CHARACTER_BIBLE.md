# Air Soldier — locked character bible

Reuse verbatim in every frame prompt. Same character as hero_ref.png. Do not redesign.

## Silhouette
- Stocky, wide-legged heavily armored infantry (same family as Fire/Water Soldier)
- Broad shoulders, thick pauldrons, heavy boots
- Full-face Corinthian-style helm with upright white-cyan wind plume
- Round buckler + slender light spear silhouette

## Palette
- Armor: pearlescent white / pale silver plate with soft cyan wind etch swirls
- Chest: large circular swirling gust/cloud core (white-cyan bright center)
- Helm visor slit: glowing pale cyan horizontal line
- Crest plume: upright white-to-cyan wispy wind brush (not fire, not water splash)
- Shield: light-metal round buckler with stylized glowing cyan GUST/spiral emblem
- Spear: pale shaft; translucent cyan-white gust-crystal tip

## Signature parts (every frame)
1. White-cyan wind plume on full-face helm
2. Glowing cyan visor slit
3. Circular swirling gust chest core
4. White/silver armor with cyan wind etch
5. Round buckler + gust/spiral emblem
6. Glowing cyan crystal-tip light spear

## Style / camera
- Cel-shaded Clash/AoE-readable game art, hard edges, high contrast
- Isometric-friendly high camera for AoE2-style play
- True transparent background; character only; no floor; no scenery; no text
- NO cape / cloak

## Facing notes
- SE = primary hero angle (matches ref)
- Unique art: N, NE, E, SE, S for idle + walk
- W / NW / SW = horizontal flip of E / NE / SE after QC
- Attack: SE primary

## Flow rules
- Idle: feet planted, spear upright-ish, shield close to body
- Walk A: left foot forward mid-stride; spear/shield follow through from idle
- Walk B: opposite foot forward; continuous cycle with Walk A
- Neighbor dirs = small turn of SAME body, not a redesign

## Gear motion (mandatory)
- Spear tip/angle moves with spear arm each frame (idle → walk A arc → walk B opposite phase)
- Round gust buckler tilts/translates with off-hand and gait
- No frozen gear; no teleport; gear always present; no appear/disappear mid-loop
